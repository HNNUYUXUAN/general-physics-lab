模型依据

$$\Delta\phi=2\pi f_0x/u,\quad\Delta f=\dot{\Delta\phi}/(2\pi),\quad k_{dyn}=4\pi^2M_{eff}/T^2,\quad k_{stat}=mg/(x-x_0).$$
